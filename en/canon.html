The Canon 藏经 · 29 full texts

Twenty-nine complete texts, each on one page: readable, citable, printable.

These are the Chinese translations, not English ones: the English column is a finding aid — title, basket, fascicle and section counts, the Taishō text number, and one line saying what the work is for. Each link opens the full Chinese text, which carries adjustable type size, a pinyin toggle, single-line repeat, and a "cite this section" control.

Three short texts recited daily — the Great Compassion Mantra, the Heart Sūtra and the Rebirth Mantra — live inside the hall rather than as separate pages, and are not counted here.
